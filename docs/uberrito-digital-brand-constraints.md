# Uberrito Digital Brand Constraints

This working document supplements the official Uberrito brand guide for website, campaign, and digital-product execution. It does not replace or alter the original brand guide.

## Background color rule

- Dark green (`#006831`) is reserved for typography, emphasis, icons, borders, and action buttons.
- Dark green must never be used as a primary page, section, navigation, modal, loader, or footer background.
- Preferred light backgrounds are Cotija White (`#ffffff`), Crispy Gray (`#e6e7e8`), Avocado Green (`#e0eed0`), and Jicama Yellow (`#f8f8d4`).
- Brand accent backgrounds may use Tomatillo Green (`#92bf38`), Cilantro Lime (`#b9d457`), Corn Yellow (`#f4c628`), Habanero Orange (`#f6932a`), and Tomato Red (`#e85025`) when contrast requirements are met.
- Dark green text and buttons must meet WCAG AA contrast against the selected light or accent background.
- Sticky navigation must use a white or translucent light-glass surface so green logo and navigation text remain legible over every section.

## Motion and performance

- Animate transforms and opacity only where practical.
- Preserve essential navigation and ordering behavior without animation.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Establish a Core Web Vitals baseline during alpha and repeat performance testing after motion is locked, before launch.
- Do not add a new animation until its mobile cost and layout stability have been reviewed.

## Imagery

- Use approved professional Uberrito photography whenever available.
- Avoid placeholder art, stretched images, baked-in promotional text, and low-resolution storefront photography.
- Maintain correct aspect ratios and provide responsive WebP or AVIF derivatives.
- Lana will select final section imagery for team review before production launch.
